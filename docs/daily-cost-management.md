# Daily Cost Management: scope and printing

## Implemented plan

1. **List-only register** (`/daily-cost`)
   - Keep top navigation, New daily sheet, Refresh, Deleted entries, well cost analysis and printing.
   - Keep well, AFE, status and date-range filters; quick/advanced grid filters; pagination; row/bulk actions; CSV/XLSX/PDF exports.
   - Remove workspace KPI cards, charts, cost-group budgets and recent activity.
   - Remove the misleading actual-to-date column calculated from the filtered register. Cumulative reconciliation remains on individual sheets and well analysis.
   - Stop requesting the workspace overview on this page; retain the existing API for compatibility.

2. **Scoped summaries**
   - Retain the existing sheet-specific daily totals and locked AFE comparison.
   - Well analysis requires one explicitly selected well; never show a multi-well summary or silently pick the first well.
   - Reject mixed AFE standard currencies in combined well analysis instead of returning a summed amount with no currency label. Individual sheets and the separated costing report remain available.
   - Preserve existing analysis capabilities. New analysis configuration, metrics and comparison policies remain open until the user supplies requirements.

3. **Print saved records**
   - The register lists **Daily total** and **Cumulative to date** side by side: the sheet's own cost, then the running actual of the same well + AFE through that date. The sheet's KPI strip shows **Cost on this date** (including the unsaved preview) beside **Cumulative cost to date**, so the day's total is known before saving.
   - **Print daily sheet** is available on a sheet to users with `daily-cost:export`. Save or discard unsaved changes first. The print reads the saved record again.
   - **Print well costing sheet** is available from the register, individual sheet and well analysis. Choose one well, inception-to-today or an inclusive date range, and whether to include drafts.
   - Inception means the first recorded cost for the well, not an assumed spud date. Future-dated sheets are excluded from inception-to-today.
   - The well report excludes soft-deleted sheets, includes empty saved sheets, and does not truncate to a grid page or inherit register search filters.
   - Period totals are separated by AFE and standard currency. There is no cross-well, cross-AFE or mixed-currency grand total in print.
   - Daily and whole-well reports print in **A4 landscape with 4 mm margins**. Page 1 follows the supplied model-report information architecture: report/well/rig identity; the milestone wording saved with the well's progress record (default **Spud date & time**, typed once per well scope in the daily sheet and re-editable) and report timing; section planned/current depth; Time Position (Planned, Actual, Actual against Planned Activities, NPT days and UPA days); service-wise and consumable-wise AFE versus cumulative actual; tangible position; overall AFE summary; section mud actual/reconciled cost; a mud matrix; and NPT/UPA cost, duration, responsible party and remarks.
   - The report title names the well scope it costs, from the AFE behind the sheets: **Well Cost Report - Drilling**, **Daily Cost Report - Completions**, or **… - Drilling & Completions** when the report covers both scopes, followed by the well name (for example `Daily Cost Report - Drilling · WELL-01`).
   - Neither the milestone label nor the lead / lag is typed at print time. The label is saved on the well scope through the sheet's **Well progress** panel; the **Lead / Lag** selection and decimal days (up to ten decimal places) are saved with the daily sheet in the **Cost report lead / lag** panel. The title ends with `(LEAD: <value> days)` or `(LAG: <value> days)`; neither prints the explicit fallback `(LEAD/LAG: 0 days)`. Editing the label or the lead / lag needs no print-time prompt and applies to every report for that well.
   - The page-1 **Services cost** and **Consumables cost** tables carry seven columns: **Cost item · AFE · Up to <previous day> · Cost on <report day> · Cumulative · Balance · Used**, so the reader sees the AFE allowance, everything spent before the report date, the cost recorded on the report date itself and the cumulative total. Currency is printed with its symbol (`$`, `€`, `£`, …) instead of the ISO code. Every AFE allowance is listed in the estimate's own reporting-group and line order, including lines with no cost yet (they print 0), so the schedule reconciles to the AFE; long schedules step down through `dcr-density-1..4` and the reporting-milestone, time-position, depth, tangible, mud and incident panels are compacted so the taller schedule still fits page one.
   - The page-1 lower row has one shared physical height and baseline without changing its placement: the bottom-left **LIH Cost** and **AFE vs Actual** panels form a double-decker whose combined height equals **Mud chemical cost · section-wise**, **Consumption / losses**, and the far-right **UPA summary**. The mud and losses panels are compacted to that UPA height; the released space is assigned to slightly taller rows in the Service and Consumable cost tables above.
   - Print presentation uses names only for rigs, wells, AFEs and all named operational/cost entities; service, consumable and tangible codes are not repeated. On the page-1 summary, services remain in the fixed Master Data reporting order and print their group name once at the start of each block, except that the fallback **Miscellaneous Services** caption is suppressed; service codes remain the hidden tie-break inside each group. Consumables print only category totals: Drill Bits is one cumulative row and the Mud Chemicals/Cement Additives labels omit "allowance". Every printed decimal is rounded and displayed to two places. This is presentation-only and does not reduce the ten-decimal stored precision.
   - The three page-1 mud tables show only the section name and mud type in their section headers—scope and section-depth suffixes are removed. The mud matrix follows the model report's upper-right layout: section and mud type are column headers; Opening Stock, Receipt, Additions, Total Mud, Losses, Closing Stock, Meterage (current depth), Cost/BBL and Reconciled Cost are rows populated from material-balance, reconciliation and daily-depth records.
   - **Consumption / Losses cumulative** is also transposed as a matrix: section and mud type are columns. Surface-loss rows are Verti-G/Mud on Cuttings, Centrifuge, By-Pass, Evaporation and Trips, followed by Surface losses total. Other-loss rows are Mud Cleaner, Formation/Seepage, Pit Cleaning/Dump, Left-In-Hole and Other losses (the row named Other losses within Consumption/Losses), followed by Other losses total and Total losses. The separate Others section—including Offline Centrifuge Loss, Backloads, OBM Left-In-Hole and its own Others row—is a material-balance deduction but is excluded from loss reporting. Historical Shakers remain included in Other losses.
   - Page 2 onward is cumulative rather than one breakdown per day: every used service is listed in the same reporting-group then AFE-estimate line order (service code only breaks ties), consumables remain exactly four category totals (Mud Chemicals, Cement Additives, Fuel and Drill Bits), and used tangible items print item-wise with their cumulative prices. Daily source records and precision remain unchanged.
   - AFE actual-to-date and balance are explicitly labelled as cumulative through the sheet date; they may include sheets outside the requested report period and include drafts under the existing reconciliation policy. They are not presented as submitted-only period totals.
   - Actual days start at the entered spud date and time. Until progress is completed, they end at the close of the last cost-sheet date included for that scope, never the date the report is printed; once a progress-completed date and time exists, its exact timestamp replaces the cost-sheet end date and freezes the count.
   - Tables print all rows using the existing AFE print layout, with repeated table headers. Choose **Save as PDF** in the browser's print dialog for a complete PDF report. Existing grid PDF exports remain unchanged.
   - Print preparation is export-audited with sheet/well scope, period and draft selection. The report API requires both `daily-cost:read` and `daily-cost:export`, and checks the authenticated workspace.

4. **Other expenses (Lost In Hole, hole problems and similar charges)**
   - Unexpected costs that have no AFE allowance — tools lost in hole, hole problems, fishing and recovery, side track, equipment damage, third-party claims, penalties — are captured in their own cost group next to Services, Consumables and Tangibles, in the table `daily_cost_other_expense_lines`.
   - Each line keeps the normal operational scope of a daily cost line: rig-scoped well, AFE, Phase, Activity, Sub-Activity and Hole Section, plus the responsible vendor or party. Classification (Planned/UPA/NPT) stays derived from the Master Data Activity; it is never typed.
   - The expense type comes from a fixed picklist (LIH, Hole Problem, Fishing & Recovery, Side Track, Equipment Damage, Third Party Claim, Penalty / Fine, Miscellaneous). It is deliberately not a master-data register: the deduction rule depends on the type, so free-text categories would make the AFE position untrustworthy. Optional free text belongs in `expense_description`.
   - **Remarks are mandatory** (at least three non-blank characters) because the remark is the only narrative that explains the charge. The rule is enforced in the API schema and as a database `CHECK` constraint, so a non-API writer cannot store an unexplained deduction.
   - The amount is `quantity × unit rate` in the entered currency, converted with the locked AFE exchange rate into the AFE standard currency — the same conversion path as every other line. A daily rate override is rejected on these lines: the entered unit rate is the price and is stored as `captured_rate` with `rate_source = other_expense`.
   - Other expenses are **included in the day's total cost** and **deducted from the locked AFE total**. Reconciliation is therefore `Total AFE − (Services + Consumables + Tangibles) − Other Expenses = AFE remaining`; `tracked_actual_to_date` and `other_expenses_to_date` keep both halves visible, `planned` for the group is always 0.00, and every sheet with a deduction carries the `other-expenses-deducted` warning plus its own audit row (`action = other_expense`) next to the save.
   - **Print selected first pages** in the register's selection bar prints only page 1 (the operational cover) of every checked daily sheet as one file, ordered by well then date, ready to print or save as a single PDF. It needs `daily-cost:export` and audits the export as `module="day-sheet", format="pdf"` with the number of pages.
   - Register, well analysis, overview, deleted entries, CSV/XLSX import (`Line Type = Other`) and the printable sheet all expose the group; the printed sheet gives it section 6 and a column in the AFE register.
   - They follow the existing sheet rules exactly: draft-only editing, submit/reopen freeze, soft delete then purge, tenant scoping on every query and permission gating on every action (`daily-cost:update` to save, `daily-cost:read` to view).

5. **Consumption and losses report** (`/reports/consumption-losses`)
   - Uses the same normalized Mud Cost entries and loss taxonomy as the printed matrix. No duplicate report store or JSON snapshot is created.
   - Supports date-wise, month-wise, selected required date range and inception views, with rig, well, section, drilling/completion scope, mud type and from/to filters. Aggregate rows drill back to their daily dates.
   - The on-screen matrix keeps sections and mud types as columns and the ten loss measures plus Surface, Other and grand totals as rows. The detail grid retains quick/advanced/date filtering, 20/50/100 pagination and CSV/XLSX/PDF exports.
   - Read access uses `daily-cost:read`; exports use `daily-cost:export` and are audited as `module="consumption_losses"`. Tenant and rig/well/section boundaries are validated by the API.

6. **Advanced consumable and tangible entry**
   - The new **Advanced selector** beside **Collect item & add another** is available when creating Consumable and Tangible lines. Consumables list every Mud Chemical; Tangibles list every tangible item. Search, row checkboxes and select-all support larger catalogues.
   - The selector displays the item, UOM and unit price from the daily sheet's locked catalogue snapshot. The user enters a positive usage quantity beside every checked item; submitting with a checked item whose usage is missing, zero or invalid keeps the selector open and identifies the item that needs correction.
   - One action creates a separate draft daily-cost line for every selected item. Those lines inherit the Activity, well Sub Activity, section/phase, actual depth and responsible party already selected in the parent dialog, preserve any previously collected items, and use the same locked-rate and FX rules as individual entry. They are committed only when the daily sheet itself is saved.

7. **LIH Cost Register worksheet** (`/daily-cost/lih-register`)
   - The main page stays register-first: select rig, well and date and press **Add items** to open the worksheet in a dialog. Active register rows use **View items** to reopen their stored lines. Items are typed as rows, duplicated from a similar item, or pasted from a block copied out of Excel; the dialog saves the whole sheet together in a single `PUT /api/v1/lih-cost/day` call instead of one request per item, then closes and refreshes the displayed total.
   - `lih_cost_entries` keeps the day header and its `revision`; `lih_cost_lines` keeps one typed row per item with its position (`sort_order`) and how it was captured (`source`: manual, duplicate, import). No JSON payload is stored and the client can never set an amount — item totals are units × unit cost and the day total is their sum, both recomputed by the server.
   - Each save is atomic and authoritative: rows with an `id` are updated, new rows are created, omitted rows are removed, and the array order becomes the saved order. A save carrying an old `revision` is rejected with **409** rather than overwriting another user's work.
   - Keyboard entry is the primary path: Ctrl+S saves, Ctrl+Enter adds a row, Ctrl+D duplicates the current row, Ctrl+I inserts below, Enter/Shift+Enter move down/up, Tab/Shift+Tab move across, and Alt+arrows move freely. Previously used descriptions fill their equipment details (never the serial number) on blur.
   - Saved rows stay editable; only the fields that change are re-sent. Days can be switched freely when the sheet is clean; while it is dirty the day selectors are locked and navigation/tab close is protected by the standard save/discard/continue-editing guard.
   - `POST /api/v1/lih-cost/import` bulk-imports up to 2,000 items for one well from `.xlsx`/`.csv`, validates every row first, groups the valid rows by their optional Date column and appends them to the matching day. Row-numbered errors appear beside the import preview while valid rows still save.
   - Permissions: `daily-cost:create` to open a day, `daily-cost:update` to save an existing day, `daily-cost:delete` to remove rows or the day, `daily-cost:import` for bulk import, `daily-cost:export` for the register and worksheet exports. Every worksheet save, bulk removal and import is audited.

## Awaiting the reference daily sheet

No reference sheet was attached to the request implemented in this increment. Existing capture fields and pricing/approval/import/delete functionality have been retained; exact reference-template parity is **not claimed**.

When the reference is supplied:

1. Inventory every header, operational section, cost row, subtotal, note and sign-off field in its printed order.
2. Map each field to existing typed columns, recording gaps explicitly.
3. Confirm units, required fields, repeatable rows and calculation rules with the user where the template is ambiguous.
4. Add only the necessary typed columns/related tables and backward-compatible migrations. Do not persist a JSON document or overwrite historical rate/budget snapshots.
5. Extend data entry, validation, import/export and this complete print layout section by section.
6. Add migration, authorization, pricing and print-parity regression tests.
